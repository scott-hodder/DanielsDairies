Welcome to Your Sensory Superpower Lab!

Your senses are superpowers

Your brain processes millions of sensory messages every day. Some brains experience sensory input differently — that’s not a problem, it can be a superpower.

This module helps you understand how YOUR brain processes sensory information and gives you tools to feel your best.

  • What makes your brain feel calm or “zappy”
  • Tools to help you regulate
  • How to create your comfort space

Daniel’s Sensory Adventure

Let’s read about Daniel’s experience at the school cafeteria…

Daniel stood at the cafeteria door and took a deep breath. Big mistake.

The smell hit him first — a weird mix of pizza, cleaning spray, and something he couldn’t identify.

Then came the noise: chairs scraping, trays clattering, kids laughing and shouting.

Daniel’s heart started beating faster. Everything felt too bright, too loud, too much.

Then he remembered his toolkit: headphones and a fidget. Slowly… the room became manageable.

He’d made it through.
