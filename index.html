Questions

Answers for parents

What ages is Daniel's Diaries for?
The program is designed for primary-school-aged children (roughly 5–13). Module content is matched to your child's age and stage, so a 6-year-old and an 11-year-old get different language, activities, and depth.
Who creates the content? Is it safe?
Content is drafted with the help of AI, then audited and reviewed by a qualified practitioner at Foundational Minds before it's approved for children. It's grounded in evidence-based psychology and behaviour support practice. Read exactly how it works on our content & review process page.
Is this therapy?
No. Daniel's Diaries is an educational wellbeing tool — it helps children learn and practise emotional skills, but it is not therapy, diagnosis, or clinical treatment, and it doesn't replace professional care. If your child needs clinical support, please speak with your GP or a qualified professional.
What happens if we don't use all our module credits?
Nothing is lost — unused credits stay in your account and roll over while your subscription is active. Busy month? Your credits will be waiting.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. You can cancel future renewals any time from your account billing settings and keep access until the end of your paid period. See the cancellation & refund policy for details.
What does Gold's behavioural support include?
Gold families are supported by Foundational Minds, led by a registered Behaviour Support Practitioner with over five years of experience in the field. Support includes guidance tailored to your child, practitioner-set tasks and follow-ups in your Family Gold hub, and help applying the Super Skills at home.
How is my child's data protected?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored with row-level security so only your family can access it, and never sold. Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe. You can export or delete your family's data at any time. Full details are in our privacy policy.
How does annual billing work?
Annual plans cost 10× the monthly price — you get 12 months for the price of 10 (2 months free). Your full year of module credits is added up front, and credits roll over, so your family can use them at their own pace.
How do I get help or contact support?
Email us at info@danielsdiaries.com — a real person from the Daniel's Diaries team reads every message, and we aim to reply within one business day.
