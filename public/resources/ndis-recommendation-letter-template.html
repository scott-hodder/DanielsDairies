NDIS Recommendation LetterThe template

Before you send this: replace every gold bracketed field, transfer the letter onto your own letterhead, and delete this box and the footer. Keep it to one page. The letter works because it makes the reasonable and necessary link in four short paragraphs: who you are, the functional impact of the child's disability, the plan goal, and why this program addresses it. Resist the urge to add more. Describe Daniel's Diaries as a structured psychoeducation program, never as therapy or treatment.

[Practitioner name and credentials]
[Role and practice name]
[NDIS practitioner ID if applicable]  ·  [Phone]  ·  [Email]
[Date]

Re: Recommendation of the Daniel's Diaries psychoeducation program for [child's full name], NDIS participant number [number]

To [plan manager / support coordinator name, or "the plan manager"],

I am a [behaviour support practitioner / occupational therapist / psychologist / role] and I have been working with [child's first name] since [month, year] in the context of [brief description of your involvement, e.g. behaviour support under their current plan].

[Child's first name] lives with [disability / diagnosis]. In day to day terms, this means [the functional impact: what the child finds hard because of their disability, e.g. recognising and naming emotions before they escalate, recovering after setbacks, reading and responding to peer interactions]. Their current plan includes the goal of [quote or closely paraphrase the relevant plan goal].

To support this goal, I recommend a [Bronze / Silver / Gold] subscription to Daniel's Diaries, a structured, evidence informed psychoeducation program that teaches children emotional regulation, resilience, and social understanding through guided weekly modules completed with adult support. I have selected this program for [child's first name] because [the specific link: which skills the program targets and how that meets the functional impact described above, e.g. it gives them structured weekly practice in noticing and naming emotional states, which is the skill their goal depends on]. The program complements the supports already in place and extends practice between sessions; it does not replace clinical services.

The subscription is billed at [$ amount] per month, which represents strong value for money against the cost of additional clinician delivered sessions targeting the same skills. I suggest an initial period of [e.g. six months], after which I will review [child's first name]'s progress and advise whether continuing is warranted.

This recommendation is provided as my professional opinion for your consideration against [child's first name]'s plan, goals, and available budget. I am happy to provide further information if useful.

Kind regards,

[Signature]
[Practitioner name, credentials, and registration details]
