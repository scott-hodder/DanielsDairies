1 You're inside the district, not another app

Tapping the Brain Builder building now means entering it. Lenny's Works Depot sits at the trailhead with a signpost back to Brain Town — same trees, same roads, same art. One world.

2 The zones live ON the road

Trailhead → Village → Town Centre → Brain City are real places along one continuous road — not an abstract progress strip. The child literally walks toward the city they're building. Roads = neural pathways stays front and centre.

3 Every module paves road and builds a building

A completed module leaves a paved segment and a little building beside it (node 1). The next module is a glowing construction site with Lenny waiting. Future road is a faint dashed trail — "not built yet".

4 The goal is visible from day one

Brain City sits pale on the horizon. Each zone unlock colours more of the skyline in. Kids always see where they're going and how the town grows because of THEIR work.

5 Characters anchored properly

Daniel walks the road (never cut off — camera keeps him in frame); the district's own guide (Lenny here, Kip for emotions, etc.) waits at the next site. No more mystery fox.

6 HUD carries the meaning

"1 of 24 roads paved" + "Next stop: Village" replaces the abstract Trailhead/Village/City strip. The old sky-floating road and duplicate background road are gone — one road, anchored to the ground.
