🧠 How your brain works

Your brain town has buildings, and each one has a job. Tap to see how it works and what you might notice in real life.

The most important thing: a child's brain is not choosing to be difficult. It is doing its best with a nervous system that is genuinely, neurologically different. Our job is to understand the map and build the roads, together.

💡 Understanding your child

Plain, strengths first explainers, with what you might see and what helps. Not labels, understanding.

A child is not a list of diagnoses. They are a whole person navigating a world that was not built for their brain.

Daniel's Diaries is an educational wellbeing tool, not therapy and not a diagnosis. Diagnosis information is for understanding and support. If you have concerns about your child, please speak with a qualified professional.
