FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. Foundational Minds is a psychoeducation and skills-building program. It’s designed to support families with practical tools and understanding. It does not replace professional mental health care.

What ages is this for?

Modules can be designed for different age ranges. The best approach is to label each module clearly (e.g. Ages 5–8, 9–12, 13–16) and guide parents to the right fit.

How long does a module take?

Most activities should be 5–10 minutes, with natural stopping points. The goal is consistency — not finishing everything in one sitting.

Can I use it with multiple children?

Yes — the experience works best when each child has their own profile so progress and stars stay personal.

How do I start?

Click “Get started” and you’ll be taken to the login page. From there you can create an account and begin with your child’s next step.

Start your child’s next step today

You don’t need to do everything at once. Even one small activity can help your child understand their feelings and build confidence over time.
